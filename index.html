About me

I moved from managing projects and producing exhibitions at Moderna Museet to a countryside lifestyle outside of Hudiksvall, where I spend my days learning programming and walking my dog Cleo.

Currently exploring all the wonders of programming .NET and trying to figure out where my heart lies within the field. Looking for internship from spring 2027, available for hire from Jan 2028.

I'm driven by a sense of belonging and would rather be a productive team member than a solo artist. Figuring out how to make things more efficient and intuitive is another main driving force.

Learning how to build something new — whether it's with code or with scrap lumper from my neighbours backyard — is what sends me on a rollercoaster ride of growth through the misery of not knowing the next step to take, followed by the thrill of figuring something out.
